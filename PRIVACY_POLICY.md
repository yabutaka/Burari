# Privacy Policy for Burari

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

Burari ("we", "our", or "the bot") is an Instagram chatbot that helps users curate a personal map of places they are interested in exploring across different cities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By messaging Burari on Instagram, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Burari through Instagram Direct Messages, we may collect:

- **Basic Instagram profile information**: your Instagram-scoped user ID, username, and profile picture, as provided to us by Meta's Instagram Messaging (Graph) API.
- **Message content**: the text of the messages you send to the bot, such as place names, city names, preferences, and requests, which we process in order to build and maintain your personal map.

We do not collect your Instagram password, and we do not have access to your private Instagram account beyond what Meta's Instagram Messaging API provides for users who message our bot.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Understand your requests and generate relevant place recommendations
- Build, update, and let you retrieve your personal saved map of places
- Improve the accuracy and relevance of the bot's responses
- Diagnose technical issues and maintain the reliability of the service

## 3. How Your Information Is Shared with Third Parties

To provide Burari's functionality, message content and related data may be shared with the following third-party service providers:

- **OpenAI**: message content is sent to OpenAI's API to interpret your requests and generate conversational responses and recommendations.
- **Google Maps (or a comparable mapping provider)**: place names, city names, and location-related queries are sent to a mapping/places API to retrieve place details, geocoding, and search results used to build your map.

These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy policies and data protection practices. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not share your data for advertising purposes.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

- Basic profile information and message content necessary to maintain your personal map are stored so that your saved places persist between conversations.
- We retain this data for as long as your account with Burari is active, or until you request deletion.
- We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

- Request a copy of the data we hold about your Burari account
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data and personal map at any time

To make any of these requests, contact us using the information below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Burari is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of Burari after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
