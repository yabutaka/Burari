Privacy Policy for Burari

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Burari helps you build a personal map of places you want to explore. It has two parts that share one account:

  • The Burari mobile app for iOS and Android, which shows your saved places on a map; and
  • The Burari Instagram account, to which you can send Instagram reels and posts about places. Burari finds the places they mention and saves them to your map.

Burari is developed and operated by Takashi Yabuta ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information Burari collects, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. It applies to both the mobile app and the Instagram account.

1. Information We Collect

From the mobile app

  • An app account identifier. On first launch the app creates an account for your installation and receives a random credential, which is kept in your device's secure storage. Our servers store only a one-way hash of this credential, never the credential itself.
  • Your chosen city. The city you pick to explore, and the city names you type into the city search.
  • Your saved places and lists. The places saved to your map, including places Burari adds automatically for a city you choose.
  • Device details when you connect Instagram. Your device platform (iOS or Android), operating system version and app version, recorded when you link the app to your Instagram account.
  • Your location, if you allow it. The app asks for your device's location so it can show where you are on the map. This is optional: the app works without it, and it is used only while you are using the app, never in the background. Your location is used on your device to draw it on the map. It is not sent to our servers, and we do not store it or keep a history of it.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

From Instagram

When you message the Burari Instagram account, Meta's Instagram Messaging API provides us with:

  • Instagram profile information: your Instagram-scoped user ID, username, display name, profile picture, follower count, verified status, and whether you and the Burari account follow each other.
  • Reels and posts you share with Burari: the link and the caption of each reel or post.
  • Connection codes: the one-time code you send to link the app to your Instagram account.

We do not store the text of other messages you send to Burari. We never receive your Instagram password, and we cannot see anything in your Instagram account beyond what Meta's Messaging API provides.

Collected automatically

  • Network information: your IP address is used to limit abuse (for example, too many requests). We store only a one-way hash of it, in short-lived counters.
  • Server logs: our servers keep technical logs of requests, such as request times, errors and the content of requests sent to the service providers listed below, to diagnose problems.

We do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To find the places mentioned in reels and posts you share, and to save them to your map
  • To show your saved places, and suggested places for the city you choose, in the app
  • If you allow it, to show where you are on the map
  • To link the app to your Instagram account so your places appear in the app
  • To reply to you on Instagram
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and diagnose and fix problems

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for anyone else.

3. How Your Information Is Shared

We share information only with the service providers that run Burari's features, and only what each needs:

  • Google Gemini (Google LLC): captions of the reels and posts you share are sent to Google's Gemini AI to identify the places they mention. We use Gemini's paid service, under which Google does not use the content to improve its products. See the Gemini API Terms.
  • Google Maps Platform (Google LLC): place names and city searches are sent to Google to look up places, and the app's map, place details and photos come from Google Maps. Google receives data such as search terms, IP addresses and map coordinates when you use these features — and, if you allow location access, your device's location, so the map can show where you are — and uses it as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Your use of Google Maps features in Burari is also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service.
  • Meta Platforms (Instagram): messages and replies travel through Meta's Instagram Messaging API. When the app shows a reel or post, it is loaded from Instagram, and Meta may collect information as described in Instagram's Privacy Policy.
  • Render (Render Services, Inc.): hosts our servers, database and cache, where the information above is stored.

These providers process information on our behalf to provide these services. We share information only with providers whose terms protect it at least as well as this policy does, and we require them to use it only to provide their services to Burari. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

4. Data Storage, Security and Retention

  • Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on servers operated by our hosting provider.
  • The app's credential and your IP address are stored only as one-way hashes. The app uses access tokens that expire after 15 minutes.
  • The app keeps a copy of your saved places on your device so the map loads quickly. Uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Your location is not stored on our servers. The app remembers only whether you chose to show it on the map.
  • Your account information, saved places, chosen city and Instagram profile information are kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete them.
  • Instagram connection codes expire after 10 minutes and are removed within about 30 minutes. Abuse-prevention counters are removed automatically after an hour.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited time by our hosting provider and then deleted.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete your account in the app: open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, saved places, lists, chosen city and Instagram connection and profile information. Reels and posts you shared may remain as public information about a place, but are no longer linked to you. The app then continues as a new, empty account.
  • Delete your data without the app: if you only use Burari on Instagram, or no longer have the app, email us from the contact below with your Instagram username and we will delete your data within 30 days.
  • Location: the app asks before using your location, and you can choose not to allow it. To stop showing your location, turn off Show my location on the map in the app's Settings, or turn off location access for Burari in your device's settings. You can allow it later from the app's Settings.
  • Disconnect Instagram: in the app's Settings, tap Disconnect to unlink the app from your Instagram account.
  • Withdraw consent: stop sending reels and posts to Burari, and delete your account as described above.
  • Access or correct your data: email us to request a copy of the data we hold about you, or to correct it.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law. Contact us to exercise them, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Age Requirement

Burari is intended only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will notify you in the app or on Instagram.
